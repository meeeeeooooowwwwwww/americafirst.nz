Designer artwork sources

Logos identify brands whose products are available from retailer Seymayka. The brands do not sponsor or endorse this website. Logo rights and trademarks remain with their respective owners. The navigation and cards are affiliate advertising linked to Seymayka.

The wordmark shapes are artwork, not copies of commercial font files. Some displayed artwork is historic and may not represent a brand's current identity; its role here is a design preview. Most source artwork is black and is displayed in pale monochrome through CSS filters.

  • Gucci: company-derived wordmark (source credited to a Gucci company PDF); historic interlocking-G emblem (Tsange). Both marked PD-textlogo/PD shape on Commons.
  • Prada: Prada wordmark, credited to Prada S.p.A., marked PD-textlogo on Commons.
  • Fendi: Fendi double-F/wordmark, credited to Fendi, from historic Fendi media; PNG rasterization used because the Commons SVG is reported invalid. Marked PD-textlogo on Commons.
  • Dolce & Gabbana: brand wordmark, vector conversion credited to Lokomotive74, marked PD-textlogo on Commons. A genuine DG-only monogram was not yet verified.
  • Versace: brand wordmark in the header (PD-textlogo) and Medusa and wordmark graphic, artwork by Viceryion, published under Creative Commons Attribution–ShareAlike 4.0. Displayed in monochrome by CSS filtering rather than changing the underlying file. Trademark rights remain applicable.
  • Moncler: group logo originating from Moncler S.p.A., marked PD-textlogo on Commons.
  • Burberry: 2023 equestrian-knight/wordmark graphic credited to Burberry Group, Commons-listed CC0/public-domain artwork.
  • Balenciaga: brand wordmark traced from printed brand media, marked PD-textlogo on Commons.

Original artwork is served from Wikimedia Commons' media host. This page provides source and licence attribution; it does not grant rights beyond the applicable file licence and trademark law.
